ABOUT

Marc Kroll

AI product manager, builder and workshop trainer in Vienna, Austria. Also known online as doyouknowmarc.

Marc Kroll is a Vienna-based AI product manager, builder and workshop trainer. He builds practical AI tools and helps people without a technical background start using AI in their everyday work. He works and teaches in German and English.

At Doxis (formerly SER Group), Kroll founded and led an internal AI Competence Center with 56 members, covering training, demos and regular exchanges across professional services, development, sales and marketing. That work earned him an internal AI Ambassador Award and led to his role as AI Product Manager. Before that he worked as a Solution Engineer and Solution Architect, and he taught digital strategy and AI workshops for teachers at IBM.

Outside his day job, Kroll builds in public. His haircut-planning app Cutmap won first place at a Lovable hackathon, and his team’s expert-knowledge agent Sensei took second place at Hack-Nation. He speaks at events such as HK Connect, and he facilitates hands-on AI workshops for small business owners and tradespeople.
